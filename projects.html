Projects

Selected research and knowledge-transfer projects in wastewater treatment, water quality, air quality, water distribution systems, dams and related applications.

Wastewater treatment and water quality

2026–2030 · Principal Investigator of subproject DC4 · Lead PhD supervisor

CeSuds — Circular Economy approaches to Digested Sludge Utilization in a Wastewater Treatment Plant Based on Hydrothermal Carbonization and Complementary Technologies

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Network under Horizon Europe. The doctoral subproject applies machine learning and large language models to optimise hydrothermal carbonisation processes for sludge valorisation. The PhD subproject is scheduled to start in February 2027.

2022–2024 · Principal Investigator

DIGIT4WATER — Digital tools for advanced wastewater treatment

Developed machine-learning models to predict pollutant removal levels in advanced tertiary treatments, including advanced oxidation processes and the formation of disinfection by-products.

Ref. TED2021-129969B-C33 · MICINN

Funded · Principal Investigator at CIMNE

RESILIENT-FLOWS — AI-enabled modelling of grey–green wastewater infrastructures

Horizon Europe project (HORIZON-CL6-2026) focused on modelling grey–green wastewater infrastructure under different climatic conditions, integrating numerical modelling and machine learning to assess and optimise wastewater treatment systems, including Nature-based Solutions.

2023–2026 · Research team member · WP3 lead

MECA-ICTUS — Computational mechanics and machine learning for urgent ischemic stroke decisions

Research on computational mechanics and machine learning to support urgent decision-making in the treatment of ischemic stroke. Responsible for WP3 on machine-learning model applications.

Ref. PID2022-136668OA-I00

2024 · Technical Coordinator · Co-developer

PRONURB — Urban air-quality forecasting

Development of urban-scale NO₂ and PM2.5 forecasting tools by coupling numerical models and artificial intelligence techniques.

Ref. ACE120/24/000126 · ACCIÓ, Nuclis d’R+D

Water distribution and air quality

2018–2020 · Technical Coordinator

SMILER — Machine learning for reducing water distribution losses

Developed a machine-learning-based system to support the reduction of water losses in distribution networks.

Ref. RTC-2017-6324-5 · Retos Colaboración 2017

2020–2023 · Technical Coordinator, Subproject 3 · Main Software Developer

PIKSEL — Territorial prediction platform

Developed a GIS-based, real-time framework to visualise, predict and analyse ozone concentrations. The operational platform was transferred to the Government of Catalonia.

PIPLATES/PIKSEL · Generalitat de Catalunya

Dams and hydraulic infrastructure

2015–2018 · Research team member · Software co-developer

ACOMBO — Thermo-mechanical analysis of arch dams

Development of software to analyse the thermo-mechanical behaviour of concrete arch dams during construction and operation. Contributed numerical solvers and the graphical user interface for the Dam Application, integrated with Kratos Multiphysics.

Ref. RTC-2015-3794-5 · Retos Colaboración 2015
