David J. Vicente

I am an Associate Research Professor at CIMNE Madrid, working in the Machine Learning in Civil Engineering (MLCE) group.

My research combines machine learning, artificial intelligence and numerical modelling for hydraulic and environmental engineering, including wastewater treatment, dam engineering, water systems and air quality.

About me

I am a Civil Engineer and hold a PhD in Civil Engineering Systems from Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. My research applies machine learning and artificial intelligence to hydraulic and environmental challenges, with work spanning wastewater treatment, water quality, water distribution, air quality and dams.
