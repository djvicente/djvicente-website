Research

My research applies machine learning, artificial intelligence and numerical modelling to hydraulic and environmental problems. The applications documented in my CV include dams, wastewater treatment, water quality, water distribution systems and air quality forecasting.

01

Dams

I contribute to dam engineering research through numerical modelling and software development, including the Dam Application for thermo-mechanical analysis. My research also includes machine-learning methods for detecting anomalous dam behaviour.

Related work: dam behaviour and monitoring publications · ACOMBO

02

Environmental Engineering

My environmental engineering work applies machine learning to water and air quality, wastewater treatment and water distribution. Topics include advanced oxidation processes, pollutant removal, disinfection by-products, surface-water quality assessment, leakage analysis and ground-level ozone forecasting.

Related projects: DIGIT4WATER · SMILER · PIKSEL
